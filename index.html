AI - Job Search Platform

CareerPilot

An agentic job-application platform - works through an approved batch overnight across LinkedIn, Greenhouse/Lever, company portals, and Workday, with firm autonomy boundaries: always pausing for sponsorship, work authorization, and anything unclear or blocked. Invite-only (runs on live AI tokens).

▸ Read the full story
Problem

Job searching means tailoring a resume and re-filling nearly identical application forms across dozens of portals - slow, repetitive, and easy to get inconsistent.

Approach

Build

Resume tailoring, ATS scoring, application tracking, an overnight agentic batch-runner across LinkedIn, Greenhouse/Lever, career pages, and Workday, plus a Chrome companion and an AI chat assistant.

In progress - invite-only, since it runs on live AI tokens. Reach out for a demo.

AI - Data Quality

Annotation Quality Lab

A labeling tool that measures whether AI training data can be trusted - inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa), gold-set accuracy, and a disagreement queue.

▸ Read the full story
Problem

Most quick labeling exercises never measure whether the labels can actually be trusted - two people can agree with each other and both be wrong.

Approach

Write a rubric with a deliberate tie-break rule and a hard "factual errors default to Poor" rule, and hold out a gold set to separate agreement from accuracy.

Build

A self-contained tool that computes Cohen's kappa, accuracy against the gold set, label distribution, and surfaces a disagreement queue for adjudication.

Outcome

Shipped and hosted - a working demonstration of the data-quality machinery behind human-in-the-loop AI.

Confidential (BCG) 🔒

Org-Design Diagnostic Skill

A Claude Skill that launches a locally-running org-design diagnostic tool - HRIS data validated on-device, ~50% faster than building the analysis manually. Built end-to-end as product owner for BCG case teams.

▸ Read the full story
Problem

Case teams without platform access were manually uploading raw client HRIS data into Claude to run diagnostics ad hoc - skipping validation and raising data-handling questions.

Approach

Scoped the ambiguity into a structured ask: defined validation rules and a hard requirement that HRIS data never leave the consultant's own machine.

Build

Partnered with engineering to validate against the real product, then built a Claude Skill launching a locally-running, Claude-built diagnostic tool - all analysis on-device.

Outcome

~50% faster diagnostics, now in active use by case teams without platform access. Details confidential to BCG.

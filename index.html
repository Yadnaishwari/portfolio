CareerPilot - the full story +
Problem

Job searching means tailoring a resume and re-filling nearly identical application forms across dozens of different portals - slow, repetitive, and easy to get inconsistent.

Approach

Design an agent with clear autonomy boundaries: safe to fill common fields on its own, but it always stops for sponsorship, work authorization, legal attestations, logins, CAPTCHAs, or a question it hasn't seen before.

Build

A platform combining resume tailoring, ATS scoring, application tracking, an overnight agentic batch-runner across LinkedIn, Greenhouse/Lever, career pages, and Workday, plus a Chrome companion and an AI chat assistant.

Outcome

In progress - invite-only, since it runs on live AI tokens. Reach out for a demo.

Annotation Quality Lab - the full story +
Problem

Most quick labeling exercises never measure whether the labels can actually be trusted - two people can agree with each other and both be wrong.

Approach

Write a rubric with a deliberate tie-break rule and a hard "factual errors default to Poor" rule, and hold out a gold set to separate agreement from accuracy.

Build

A self-contained tool that computes Cohen's kappa, accuracy against the gold set, label distribution, and surfaces a disagreement queue for adjudication.

Outcome

Shipped and hosted - a working demonstration of the data-quality machinery behind human-in-the-loop AI.

Org-Design Diagnostic Skill - the full story +
Problem

Case teams without platform access were manually uploading raw client HRIS data into Claude to run diagnostics ad hoc - skipping validation and raising data-handling questions.

Approach

Scoped the ambiguity into a structured ask: defined validation rules and a hard requirement that HRIS data never leave the consultant's own machine.

Build

Partnered with engineering to validate against the real product, then built a Claude Skill launching a locally-running, Claude-built diagnostic tool - all analysis on-device.

Outcome

~50% faster diagnostics, now in active use by case teams without platform access. Details confidential to BCG.
